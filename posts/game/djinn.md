    <p>
        For this game jam, my goal was to try my hand again at another simple puzzle game. I wanted to focus in a core mechanic and instead of messing around with multiple mechanics just try to see what levels I can make with what I had. I also made use of my "Dreamspace" palette and it worked out well!
    </p>
    <p>
        I was able to churn out about 15+ levels that I felt mostly satisfied with, exploring a few mechanics centered around the core mechanic with the djinns coupled with some basic block pushing mechanics. There was a few levels that ended up with tedious mechanics / puzzles that I felt didn't really feel satisfying or intuitive to play and solve.
    </p>
    <p>
         Overall I'm happy with the results, the art being cohesive and coherent enough, the music was not too grating on the ears, puzzles are alright and while the programming was a bit sloppy with some funny bugs it was nothing too gamebreaking.
