    <p>
        Made in 72 hours for a private game jam with friends.
    </p>
    <p>
        This my attempt at experimenting with a different visual style using shaders and noise and such. I also tried a bit of using noise for procedural
        terrain generation. Going further into a less pure pixel art style was something I've rarely done but I'm glad it turned out well here!
    </p>
    <div class="media"><img src="/public/img/gallery/games/inkwell/2.png" alt="Main menu."></div>
    <p class="caption">A monster of the depths</p>
    <p>
        Gameplay-wise the player controls a slow and somewhat fragile scouting ship trying to navigate the dark and rocky depths of the inkwell. I implemented
        an FTL-like system of energy management where the ship's facilities function better with higher energy allocation. Though later on in playing it felt
        clunky to play with but nevertheless I liked the variety it brought to how you played the game.
    </p>
