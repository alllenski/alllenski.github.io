inkwell

into the ink sea

The ship's descent

Made in 72 hours for a private game jam with friends.

This my attempt at experimenting with a different visual style using shaders and noise and such. I also tried a bit of using noise for procedural terrain generation. Going further into a less pure pixel art style was something I've rarely done but I'm glad it turned out well here!

A monster of the depths

Gameplay-wise the player controls a slow and somewhat fragile scouting ship trying to navigate the dark and rocky depths of the inkwell. I implemented an FTL-like system of energy management where the ship's facilities function better with higher energy allocation. Though later on in playing it felt clunky to play with but nevertheless I liked the variety it brought to how you played the game.
