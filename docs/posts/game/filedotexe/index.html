Viruses infect your pc!

Made in 96 hours for GMTK Game Jam 2024 "Built to Scale."

This game jam was done in collaboration with @riiemi doing the design and pixel art! Our game focused on messing around with rescaling windows and various things you find in your infected computer. Find out a way to navigate the annoying ads by rescaling them and finally unlock the admin window.

Even more viruses

Programming the scaling windows and items was quite troublesome and ended up being buggy (but playable.) Nevertheless it was a good experience and also served as a proof of concept for windows I eventually used in my overlay!
