What's happening?

Welcome to the third redesign of my website! This will hopefully be the last [probably not] redesign... There are a couple reasons why I decided to redesign my website. I will go over them and my future plans in general as well :D

Blargh!

I've decided to try a little blog thing to ramble about random thoughts! This'll most likely be about the site, game dev, tech, pixel art or anything really. Another outlet for me to practice my writing as I do like writing whatever :D

New style!

I've settled on this blue-ish green-ish palette with clean round shapes as the general look for the site. This palette was originally made for the previous redesign and from there I used the palette further in some of my games! I'm generally happy with this palette and found it limited but still quite versatile enough for my creative needs.

A landing page

For some time my online presence has mostly been through posting pixel art and game dev on Twitter but I've been wanting to create my own little corner for a while, and so here it is! It also serves as a convenient glimpse into my works as well as other socials I've been branching out to!

Learning more stuff

One of the reasons I decided to redo my website was due to how I manually created posts and content for the previous website which was getting cumbersome. This time I decided to try a simple static site generator!

What's next?

I'll continue to add more to the site as I go but for now I'll continue working on my projects so I have stuff to write about!
